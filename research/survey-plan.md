# DayStead survey plan

**Goal:** learn whether people really have the problem DayStead solves, and whether they would pay. Past behaviour counts more than opinions: "did you miss a bill?" beats "would you use an app?".

**Form:** run `research/survey-form.gs` in Google Apps Script. It creates the Google Form and a responses sheet.

**Who to ask:** unpaid carers (carers' Facebook groups, local carers' centres), people paid monthly who struggle before payday, adults with ADHD. Friends and family are polite, so count them separately.

**Target:** 50 or more responses.

**How to read the results**
- Good sign: most people answer yes to the missed bill or missed dose questions, describe real pain in the free-text answer, and at least 1 in 5 leave an email.
- Warning sign: "nice idea" answers, few recent problems, few emails.
- The emails become the 12 or more Google Play closed testers needed before launch.

**After the survey:** the CEO agent reviews the responses sheet (download as CSV and add it to `research/`, with emails removed) and recommends whether to go ahead, change the audience, or change the product.
